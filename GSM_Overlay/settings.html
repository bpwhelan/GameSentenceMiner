VN Guide

Follow a route from kanjieater’s VN-Guide at the top left. Only the current step is shown. Guides are downloaded on demand, cached locally, and credited to VN-Guide; your game text stays on your computer.

Enable the helper and choose a game guide.

Save steps briefly notify you, then stay with the next choice as a reminder to save before choosing. Advancing that choice clears both instructions. Outside Always mode, a card opened with Show / Hide hides after two new game lines. Hiding does not suppress the next automatically detected choice. Loads, and saves without a following choice, wait for Done.

The guide stays available when manual mode hides the lookup overlay. Detection needs overlay OCR coordinates with multiple separated options on screen. Short dialogue matches, text hooks alone, and uncertain layouts do not trigger it. Use the hotkey or Always mode when needed; manual mode may need a scan at the choice screen.
