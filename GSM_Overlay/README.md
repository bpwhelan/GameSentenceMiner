# Overview

https://github.com/bpwhelan/GameSentenceMiner

This Overlay is designed to take plaintext and coordinates output from GSM to both display in a main textbox, as well as on-screen lookups overtop of the characters on screen.

This is practically a completely different project from the original, and such, it cannot be used standalone, and should NOT be merged with the original project.

# Development
- Run `npm run dev` in `GSM_Overlay` to start Vite + Electron with hot reload for overlay HTML/CSS/JS and automatic Electron restarts for main/preload script changes.

# Custom pages
- Open **Overlay Settings → Interaction → Custom Page**, paste an `http://` or `https://` URL, and enable the option. For example, use [VN-Guide](https://kanjieater.github.io/VN-Guide/) or a specific game's walkthrough.
- While the game is available to the overlay, press **Alt+Shift+U** (configurable) to show or hide the page. **Escape** returns to the game, **Alt+Left/Right** goes back/forward, and **Ctrl+R** or **F5** reloads.
- This is off by default and loads nothing until opened. Hiding keeps your place. Closing and reopening the overlay resumes the last page visited for each configured URL, including route fragments; the website's saved progress also persists across GSM restarts. The URL and hotkey follow overlay profiles when those are enabled.

# VN Guide helper
- Open **Overlay Settings → Interaction → VN Guide**, enable the helper, and paste a specific game page from [kanjieater’s VN-Guide](https://kanjieater.github.io/VN-Guide/), or choose **Use current Custom Page**. The import button also imports the page’s route and step when present in its URL. Select a route in settings; step numbers in GSM start at 1.
- **Guide appearance → Plain text (click-through)** shows just the current instruction at the top left, without a box or controls. It cannot take focus or intercept mouse clicks. Use the guide hotkeys or settings to navigate; visibility and automatic hiding work the same as the card. The default **Card with controls** includes route details and save reminders. Appearance follows overlay profiles.
- **Alt+Shift+G** toggles the top-left card, **Alt+Shift+Right** advances/confirms, and **Alt+Shift+Left** goes back/undoes. All three shortcuts are configurable. Outside Always mode, showing the card is a temporary preview: it hides after two fresh game lines, using the existing text feed or final OCR results. Repeated scans, revisions of the same OCR line, supplemental scans, and capture interruptions do not consume preview lines. A detected choice stays visible while its menu remains on screen. Hiding a preview or choice does not suppress the next automatically detected choice. The card has Back/Next/Done buttons; settings also offer a numbered jump without revealing future choices. Completing a route stops there so you can choose the next route yourself.
- Automatically advancing a choice shows a compact **Choice {choice} logged** confirmation using the previous answer. It hides after four seconds, or stays visible in Always mode until another choice is detected or you navigate manually. The confirmation contains no upcoming instruction, route details, step count, ending label, or completion message. From a confirmation, **Alt+Shift+Right** reveals the pending instruction without consuming it, and **Alt+Shift+Left** undoes the automatic advance and reveals the original choice. This also works after the confirmation fades. Manual navigation shows the instruction directly and uses the ordinary preview behavior. The next detected choice shows its recommendation normally. Use the guide toggle to explicitly preview upcoming instructions or reveal a standalone save/load action. Hotkey-only mode keeps automatic confirmations hidden unless the guide was already open.
- Choose automatic visibility, always-visible, or hotkey-only. In automatic mode, loading or jumping to a save before a choice shows an eight-second notice to save at your earliest convenience, ideally at that next choice. Once the choice is detected, its card shows both the save instruction and the recommended answer. Always-visible and hotkey-only cards show both together. Advancing the choice clears its save reminder; **Back** restores both. Closing the brief notice does not suppress the later choice reminder. Loads, and saves without a following choice, still wait for **Done**. Hiding the card does not mark a step complete. The card only appears while the overlay has a usable game capture, and hides for Custom Page/Text Feed or an unavailable game.
- Detection checks both incoming GSM text and existing overlay OCR, without additional polling or uploads. OCR works even when the scan has no matching texthook text. Comparisons remove punctuation, symbols, spaces, and menu prefixes while preserving letters (including Japanese ー) and the original displayed guide text. Incoming text can reveal the current instruction through an exact, unique whole-line match; it cannot advance progress by itself. OCR also tolerates one or two character errors in longer choices when lengths are similar and the text occupies its own block. A similarly sized, aligned option and a central position add confidence, but the text can appear anywhere on screen. Short answers such as 大丈夫 still require an exact match and a separate option. Ambiguous near-matches, substantial length differences, text embedded in paragraphs, and supplemental scans do not qualify for fuzzy detection. A lone matching block can reveal a hint, but cannot arm automatic advancement. Manual mode may require an overlay scan at the choice screen; always-visible/hotkey modes work without choice detection.
- Manual mode’s **Hide overlay** setting only hides the lookup overlay: the guide remains available over the game between lookups and returns above the lookup window when it opens again, including freeze-frame backgrounds. Temporary capture interruptions, Custom Page, and Text Feed hide the guide without forgetting its visibility, current choice, or save reminder; notice timers pause while capture is unavailable. Automatic hints return as **Last detected choice** until fresh OCR or backend revalidation confirms the menu. Old evidence cannot advance progress across a capture interruption. A different game clears that remembered detection.
- Optional automatic advance waits for a fresh final OCR frame where the entire detected menu has disappeared. Recognizable save/load headings or overwrite prompts pause progression until the choice returns, so opening a save screen does not consume the choice. It is **tentative**: OCR cannot confirm which option was clicked or whether you saved. **Back** undoes an advance and restores its save reminder. Disabling automatic advance leaves every choice and standalone save/load action manual.
- With automatic advance enabled, a fresh final OCR result can also recover a missed advance by exactly matching one unique menu within the next **three choices**. Fuzzy matches and lone blocks cannot trigger catch-up. The current instruction takes priority; repeated answers, multiple future matches, known alternatives in the old menu, save/overwrite screens, and stale or supplemental results do not trigger catch-up. Loads stop the search. A save may be included with its following choice and its reminder is shown immediately, but the search cannot skip past that choice; an existing save reminder limits catch-up to the next choice. The card marks **Caught up from step …**, and **Back** restores the position before the whole jump. No future instructions are displayed until matched.
- Only VN-Guide’s `guide.json` plus ordered `route_<id>.json` / `simpleJp` format is supported. The official game page, GitHub forks (including renamed repositories), GitHub file/folder URLs, and raw JSON URLs work. GitHub Pages game URLs use the repository’s `main` branch; GitHub and raw URLs can select other branches. For local guides, use **Choose local folder** to select the game folder containing `guide.json` and its route files, or paste its absolute path, `guide.json` path, or `file:` URL. Every source uses the same manifest and route validation and file size limit. Guide HTML and scripts are never executed. Arbitrary walkthrough sites are unsupported. Routes retain source order, including documented detours. Instructions and source review status come from the selected guide; GSM does not rewrite or redistribute the guides.
- **Available guides** loads kanjieater’s `games.json` catalogue by default. Search by title, alternate title, target edition, or folder name, then select a guide to enable it for the current scene. **Catalogue source** accepts another GitHub repository, a branch URL, a raw `games.json` URL, or a local root folder containing the same `games.json` format. GitHub repository roots use `main`; select a branch explicitly for other refs. The catalogue source is shared across scenes and profiles; each scene retains its selected guide independently. **Use default catalogue** restores kanjieater’s repository, and **Refresh catalogue** downloads the latest list. Catalogues are cached separately in `vn-guide-catalogue-cache.json`; a failed refresh retains the last valid list. Only entries marked `has_guide: true` are shown, and selecting a guide still applies the existing manifest and route validation. Direct guide URLs and individual local game folders remain available below the selector.
- Imports are cached in `vn-guide-cache.json`, and independent per-source/game/route progress is saved in `vn-guide-progress.json` under the overlay data directory. Forks, branches, and local copies keep separate progress; existing official-guide progress is retained. Cached routes work offline. Save reminders also survive restarts and manual jumps. Original guide step numbers are retained: jumping to a save or its following choice shows both instructions. GSM checks the guide manifest and selected route for updates when opened and hourly while enabled; **Check for updates** checks immediately, including edits to local files. Available updates appear in settings and on the guide card, without changing the current instructions, cached guide, or progress. **Update guide from source** (or **Reload guide from source**) applies the latest content; changed instructions reset the position and show a notice, while metadata-only changes preserve progress. Failed checks leave the cached guide usable and retry hourly. GSM’s progress is separate from the website’s local storage.
- Guide URL/folder, selected route, and enabled state are saved per OBS scene, even when several scenes share a GSM profile or overlay profiles are disabled. Switching scenes restores that scene’s guide selection automatically. Existing global/profile guide selections migrate to the first scene observed for each selection; new scenes otherwise start with no guide. Selections made before a scene is known are attached to the first observed scene. Appearance, visibility mode, automatic advance, and shortcuts continue to follow overlay profiles when enabled. Step progress remains associated with the guide source and route, so choosing the same guide in two scenes shares its position.

# Controller quick actions

While controller navigation is active, press **Start** (or **Tab** for keyboard navigation) to open Quick actions. Use the D-pad or either analog stick to move, your configured Confirm button to select, and Cancel to return. Closing the panel keeps your text selection. With modifier activation, keep holding the navigation modifier.

The default panel offers pause/resume text intake, replay the latest line, show/hide furigana, and Translate & explain. The latter includes the Text Feed's sentence breakdown, grammar, vocabulary, nuance and tone, scene summary, and custom question. Long explanations scroll with the D-pad or stick. Audio uses Text Feed's native playback, including its cache and built-in player.

In **Controller → Quick actions**, remap the opening button or combo, select and reorder shortcuts, pin individual explanation types, and save a custom question that can be asked without typing during play. These settings follow overlay profiles. Text intake pause is independent of game/process pausing and remains in effect after the menu closes.
