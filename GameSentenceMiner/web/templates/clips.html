Make Cards From Your Clips

Clips let you make Anki cards at your own pace, after the OBS replay buffer has moved on

  1. Look up a word in one of the clips below with Yomitan, and add the card to Anki.
  2. GSM recognises the clip and adds its audio and screenshot, with the usual confirmation dialog on the desktop.
  3. If that doesn't happen (for example, Anki isn't connected to GSM), press ✨ Enrich latest card on the clip.

Save clips from the Text Feed with 💾 Save clip for later. A clip stays here after you make a card from it, so you can make more cards from the same sentence.

⚠️ Each clip is a video file, often 10–40 MB, so this isn't meant as a long-term collection of sentences. Once you've made your cards, delete the clips you no longer need.
