# What's Changed in 2026.10.2

A new efficient OCR option and a few everyday improvements.

## VOCR: lightweight and promising

**VOCR** is a local OCR engine for horizontal Japanese dialogue. Its models are just **0.82–2.48 MB**. It's opt-in; your existing OCR defaults stay the same. 

Thanks to [bee-san](https://github.com/bee-san) for [vita-vn-ocr](https://github.com/bee-san/vita-vn-ocr), the original VOCR project behind GSM's integration.

In four **Akai Ito** gameplay recordings, VOCR Large matched **522 of 540 hooked lines (96.7%)**, with **0.45% character error** on **hooked** text after ignoring punctuation and spacing. These time-paired results cover one game; missed lines and occasional pairing errors still need work. Early days, but very promising.

Choose **OCR → Advanced → Recognition → Main OCR → VOCR**. GSM downloads its runtime and model on first use; later scans work offline. Overlay OCR can use it too. Model weights are for non-commercial use. [Setup and limits](https://github.com/bpwhelan/GameSentenceMiner/blob/main/docs/VITA_VN_OCR.md).

It's also worth trying using VOCR Text Detector alongside Google Lens with super fast text detection, while maintaining the excellent accuracy of Google Lens.

## Also in this update

- **GPU settings:** check NVIDIA setup and choose CPU/GPU use separately for MeikiOCR, FireRedVAD, and Whisper.
- **Unwanted text:** exclude exact captured entries per game from the OCR feed, or manage them under **Text Processing → Excluded sentences**.
- **VN Guide:** browse available guides and remember the selected guide and route per game.
- **VN Guide:** fix "Choice Logged" Showing until the next choice is detected in "Always show" mode.
- Fix FireRedVAD audio preprocessing. Sampled a few times where the end of the audio wasn't detected properly and it turns out it was because of the audio preprocessing we were doing. This also gives a nice little speed bump in showing the confirmation dialogue. If you moved to silero it might be worth another shot.

A little Halloween decoration, too. **Settings → General → Disable seasonal effects** turns it off.
